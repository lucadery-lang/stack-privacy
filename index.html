Stack

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

Stack is built on a simple promise: your data stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking. We — the developer of Stack — never receive, store, or see your information. Nothing you enter is transmitted off your iPhone to us or to anyone else.

In short: Stack collects nothing from you. Everything you log lives only on your device, under your control, and is deleted when you remove the app.

What Stack stores, and where

Stack is a personal tracking log. When you use it, you may create information such as:

  • Doses and protocols you log (compound, amount, units, schedule, reconstitution math, injection sites, titration notes)
  • Bloodwork and biomarker values, and body-composition or DEXA scan data you choose to import
  • Progress photos you choose to take or add
  • App settings, reminders, and preferences

All of this is saved in a local file inside the app's private storage area on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. Stack has no server, no cloud database, and no developer-side storage of any kind.

No accounts, no networking, no tracking

  • No accounts or sign-up. You don't create an account, log in, or give us an email to use Stack.
  • No servers. Stack does not send your data to us or to any third party. It performs no network requests to transmit your information.
  • No analytics or telemetry. We do not measure how you use the app, collect crash logs, or profile you.
  • No advertising. Stack contains no ads and no advertising networks.
  • No third-party SDKs or trackers. Stack does not embed third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software.
  • No selling or sharing. Because your data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to sell, rent, or share. We do not.

This is why Stack's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant permission, Stack can read data from Apple Health — such as weight, body composition, and clinical health records like lab results — so it can show you correlations alongside what you log.

  • Health access is read-only. Stack never writes to Apple Health.
  • Health data is used only on your device, solely to display trends and correlations for you.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never shared with the developer or any third party.
  • You can grant or revoke Health permissions at any time in the iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health screen.

This use complies with Apple's HealthKit requirements, including the rule that HealthKit data is never used for advertising or sold.

Camera and photos

Stack uses the camera only for tasks you initiate:

  • Scanning a vial or prescription label, read on-device with Apple's Vision text recognition.
  • Taking progress photos that you choose to capture or add.

Photos and scan images are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, never sent to the developer, and never shared. Progress-photo images are excluded from iCloud backup and are never included in any data you export from the app. Your DEXA results are part of your on-device log: they stay on your device and leave it only through your own iPhone backup or a data export you deliberately create.

Estimated levels and sources

Stack estimates medication levels using published pharmacokinetics from sources like FDA prescribing information and peer-reviewed studies. These estimates are calculated on your device from what you log. They are clearly labeled as estimates (prefixed "≈"), with the future projection drawn dashed, and are not lab measurements. Displaying them involves no data collection.

Deleting your data

You are always in control of your data:

  • Use the in-app delete options to remove specific entries or clear your data.
  • Deleting the Stack app from your device removes all of its stored data, including logs, imported values, and photos.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Stack is not directed to children. It is intended for adults managing their own health tracking. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone — including children — because we do not collect information at all.

Not medical advice

Stack is a personal tracking and education tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not recommend, prescribe, sell, or source any substance. Estimated levels and trends are informational — "a reason to look, not a verdict." Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health and any medications or compounds.

Stack

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Stack stores your tracking record on your device. It has no account system, developer-operated cloud storage, advertising or tracking SDKs. Stack does not automatically upload your health record to us.

You can choose to export or share information. Apple services, including device backups, Health and TestFlight, handle information as described below.

Your tracking record

Stack stores the doses, schedules, vial information, notes, lab results, body measurements, progress photos and settings you enter or import. Your record is kept in the app's private storage using iOS file protection. Widgets use a limited summary shared locally with the app.

The current app does not send your record to a hosted AI service. Stack has no advertising or tracking SDKs and does not sell your health information.

Apple Health

With your permission, Stack can read supported measurements from Apple Health, including weight, body fat, lean mass, sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate. A separate permission allows access to supported clinical lab records.

If you allow writes, Stack can save body-composition values you create in Stack to Apple Health: weight, body fat percentage and lean mass. It does not write clinical records or copy imported Health samples back into Health.

Access is optional. You choose the data types Stack may read and write in Apple's permission controls and can change them in Settings. Health information is used to show your record, trends and comparisons; it is not used for advertising.

Imported Health values can be included in exports or summaries you deliberately share. Apple's own storage, sync and backup settings apply to information held in Health.

Camera and progress photos

Stack uses the camera for actions you choose, such as reading a label, taking a progress photo or measuring your pulse. Pulse measurement does not record or retain photos or video.

Progress photos are stored locally and excluded from the app's device backup and its record-export files. You can deliberately share them separately from the photo gallery. A copy saved to Photos, Files or another destination is then managed by that destination.

Exports, sharing and backups

You can export your record or share a summary using the system share sheet. These files may contain health information, including imported labs and body measurements. Stack sends the files to the destination you choose; that destination's privacy and storage practices apply.

Your ordinary app record may be included in an Apple device backup, depending on your settings. Progress photos are excluded. Keep a record-export JSON file if you want a separate copy that Stack can restore.

TestFlight and support

If you use the TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. Feedback you submit, including comments or screenshots, is also shared and may identify you. See Apple's TestFlight privacy notice.

If you contact support, we receive the information you send, such as your email address, message and attachments. We use support and diagnostic information to answer requests and investigate problems. Contact us about access to or deletion of information you have sent us.

Deleting information

Use Stack's delete controls to remove information from your current record. Uninstalling the app removes its on-device app storage. Exported or shared copies, device backups, values saved to Apple Health and support or TestFlight submissions are separate; uninstalling Stack does not delete them. Manage those copies at their destination.

About Stack

Stack is intended for adults managing their own tracking. It is an informational tool, not a source of diagnosis or treatment. Medication-level estimates are calculated on your device from the information you log and configured assumptions; they are not lab measurements. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about your care.
